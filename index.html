Passion Project

Unfortunate Tales of Violet

An action-adventure game where players draw runes in real-time to cast spells in a world full of dark humor and absurdity. Developed over two years, focusing on creating a uniquely chaotic spellcasting experience blending combat and animation.

  • Developed over two years as a two people team, reaching the vertical slice stage with a focus on creating a uniquely chaotic spellcasting experience that blends fast-paced combat with cartoon animation.
  • Since drawing accurately in fast-paced action is challenging, we designed the shapes to be simple and flexible. The game features a doubling combination system that guides players toward mastery.
  • We use Unity 6 game engine and FMOD sound engine.

Who Sank The Titanic?

Multiplayer social deduction game set in 1912. Time travelers try to save the Titanic from disaster while hidden saboteurs work secretly to ensure history remains unchanged.

  • Who Sank The Titanic? is a multiplayer social deduction game set during the Titanic’s final day on the North Atlantic Ocean in 1912.
  • Players play as time travelers trying to save the Titanic from disaster while keeping the passenger’s satisfaction high, but hidden imposters work secretly to ensure history remains unchanged by sabotaging tasks, reducing the satisfaction and turning players against each other.
  • We use Unity 6 game engine, Mirror for multiplayer network and FMOD sound engine.
